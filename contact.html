FAQ

Before You Reach Out

Is the discovery call really free?
Yes — 100% free with zero obligation. We'll understand your business, ask the right questions, and share an honest view of how we can help. You decide whether to proceed. No pressure, ever.
What if I'm not sure what I need?
That's exactly why we have the discovery call. Just tell us your business challenge and your goal. We'll help you figure out the right solution — whether that's a website, ads, AI automation, or something else entirely.
How quickly will you respond?
We respond within 1 hour on WhatsApp and within 24 hours on email during business hours (Mon–Sat, 10AM–7PM IST). For urgent needs, WhatsApp is the fastest channel.
Do you sign NDAs before discussing my project?
Absolutely. We sign NDAs and work agreements before any project begins. Your ideas, business information and strategy are completely safe with us. Transparency is a core value at Upfigure.
Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes — our team is based across Gurugram, Kanpur and Bhopal, and we work with clients across India, USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia and globally. All communication happens via WhatsApp, Zoom and email. Location is never a barrier.
What happens after I submit this form?
We review your details, do some initial research on your business and market, then reach out to schedule a discovery call. In that call, we listen more than we talk. After, we send you a clear proposal tailored to your specific situation.
