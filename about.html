We Started With a Mission

We are a full-service creative agency based in Gurugram, helping brands grow through design, technology, and marketing. Born out of a passion for building impactful brands, Upfigure was created to give businesses access to world-class creative services under one roof.

We believe great design is not just about aesthetics — it's about results. Every project we take on is executed with strategy, creativity, and a laser focus on your business goals.
